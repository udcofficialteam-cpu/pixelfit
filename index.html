GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. PixelFit uses your browser to process the image. The image itself is not sent to PixelFit's server. As with any website, the static page still needs to load from its hosting provider.

Can I make an image exactly 100 KB?

PixelFit can try to get the file at or below a target size by adjusting compression and, if you enable it, dimensions. Exact size is not always possible at the same dimensions without visible quality loss. Always check the final file size before submitting a form.

Which formats are supported?

The current version supports common JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP outputs. GIF and BMP may be accepted as source images when your browser can decode them, but output is a still image. HEIC/HEIF, TIFF and animated output formats are not supported in this initial version.

Will a wallpaper fit my exact monitor?

Choose a listed resolution or enter your monitor's exact width and height. Fill & crop fills the whole frame but may cut off edges; Fit entire image keeps the whole picture and may add margins.
