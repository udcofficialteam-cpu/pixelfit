Privacy Policy

We aim to keep image processing on your device and explain the third-party services needed to deliver PixelFit.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Overview

This policy explains how PixelFit handles information when you visit the website. PixelFit is a static, browser-based image toolkit and does not require an account.

Your images and local processing

For the image tools, the selected image is processed in your browser. PixelFit does not upload your selected image to a PixelFit image-processing server. Resizing, cropping, compression, format export and the background-removal inference are performed on your device.

The Background Remover downloads its browser-side code and AI model from a public content delivery service when needed. Your browser requests those files from that service; the photo itself is processed locally. The AI model or related assets may be stored in your browser cache. Clearing browser data or storage may mean downloading the model again.

Hosting and technical information

The website is delivered by a hosting provider. Like other websites, the hosting and content-delivery services may process technical request information needed to deliver, maintain and protect the site (for example, request and diagnostic data). Their handling of such information is governed by their own privacy notices.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

PixelFit currently focuses on browser-based image tools and does not require an account. If PixelFit displays ads using Google AdSense, Google and third-party advertising vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, limit, personalize and measure ads, report performance, and help detect invalid activity.

Google's advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to show ads to visitors based on their previous visits to PixelFit and/or other websites. Visitors can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Visitors may also review choices for some third-party vendors' personalized advertising cookies at aboutads.info/choices. For more information about Google's use of data on partner sites, see Google's partner-site data policy.

If other advertising networks or analytics providers are enabled, this policy may be updated to identify them and link to their applicable privacy notices. Third-party providers manage their own cookies and technologies; PixelFit does not control them. Where required by applicable law or provider policies, appropriate consent notices and choices will be shown before relevant technologies are used.

Third-party services

PixelFit may rely on third-party hosting and public CDNs to serve website files and the Background Remover model. PixelFit does not control the privacy practices of those providers; please review their policies when you use their services.

Children and accounts

PixelFit does not ask visitors to create an account or submit personal profile information. If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to include in that message.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website’s features, providers or legal requirements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
