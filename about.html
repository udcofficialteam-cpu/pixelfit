ABOUT PIXELFIT

Image tools made simpler.

PixelFit brings everyday image resizing, compression, conversion, wallpaper sizing and background removal together in one simple website.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

What is PixelFit?

PixelFit is a free, browser-based image toolkit for everyday image tasks. It helps visitors resize and crop images, prepare social media graphics and desktop wallpapers, compress files for upload limits, convert common image formats, and remove image backgrounds.

Built for simple, practical use

Choose a preset or enter your own dimensions, preview the result, and download it to your device. PixelFit does not require an account or a PixelFit API key. Common image editing and export operations run locally in your browser.

Background removal

The Background Remover loads a browser-based AI engine and model when you first use it. The engine/model files may be requested from a public CDN and may be cached by your browser for later use. Your selected photo is processed on your device rather than uploaded to PixelFit.

Limitations

Results depend on the source image, browser, device memory and supported formats. File-size targets are best-effort, and an output size is not guaranteed for every image. Social platform dimensions are reference presets and may change; always check the destination platform or official application portal for its current requirements.
